Privacy Notice

Who is responsible?
For this booking, Bola, operating Bola Racket Service, is the data controller. You can contact the service through the same WhatsApp conversation used for the booking.

What we use your information for

  • to receive and manage the stringing request;
  • to contact you about drop-off, setup questions and collection;
  • to complete and administer the requested racket service; and
  • only if you choose “Keep my racket setup on file”, to make future repeat bookings easier.

Legal basis
Booking and contact information is processed because it is necessary to take steps at your request and provide/administer the service. Where limited information relates to a player other than the person making the booking, it is used only as necessary to operate and complete that requested racket service. Optional saved setup history is retained only when you choose that feature and can be deleted on request.

What we collect
Player name, age group, parent/member contact, racket details, service request, playing frequency, string-breakage information, string/tension preferences, timing and relevant service notes. Please do not include medical, injury or other sensitive personal information in the notes.

WhatsApp
When you press “Send booking via WhatsApp”, the booking information is transferred to WhatsApp. WhatsApp/Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy arrangements. This webpage does not itself send the booking to a separate Bola Racket Service database.

How long we keep it
Operational booking information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed to manage the service and any applicable legal/accounting requirements. If you choose to keep your racket setup on file, the limited setup history will be kept for up to 24 months after the last service, unless you ask for it to be deleted sooner.

Marketing
Booking details will not be used for unrelated promotional or direct-marketing messages unless a separate marketing opt-in is obtained.

Your rights
You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information held by the service, object to or restrict certain processing where applicable, or withdraw an optional saved-setup request. You may also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission if you believe your data-protection rights have been infringed.

Junior bookings: where the player is under 16, the booking/contact should be made by a parent, guardian or other responsible adult.
